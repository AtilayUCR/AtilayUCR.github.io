Privacy Policy

Qibla Finder · Last updated 18 November 2026

Qibla Finder shows the direction of the Kaaba and prayer times for where you are. This policy explains what data the app handles. The app is published by Mujdat Atilay Ucar (“we”).

In short: no account, no sign-up, and we do not sell your data. The app's own data stays on your device. Ads (Google AdMob) and anonymous usage/crash statistics (Google Firebase) are the only third-party services, and ad personalisation follows your consent.

Data stored on your device

No account is needed. Your settings are stored only on your device.

Location: with your permission, your location is used on your device to calculate the Qibla direction and prayer times. We do not send your location to our own servers or store it. If you open the map, map images for the area you view are loaded from the map provider (OpenFreeMap), which receives the requested map tiles and your IP address like any website.

Camera: with your permission, the camera is used only to show the Qibla direction over the live image. Images are not recorded or sent anywhere.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging issues a device token so we can send reminders. You can turn notifications off in your device settings at any time.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device model and OS, and ad interactions.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ad is requested you are shown Google's consent message (a certified consent management platform under IAB TCF). You can accept, decline, or manage options. If you decline, you only see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the app.
  • iOS: the app asks for App Tracking Transparency permission. If you do not allow tracking, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised with it.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.

Google's use of this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand which features are used (for example: screens opened, a dhikr set completed, the app language) and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports (stack trace, device model, OS version, app version). This data is tied to a random app-instance identifier, not to your name, e-mail or phone number. Advertising-related analytics follow your consent choice above (Google Consent Mode). Details: Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Ads are configured not to be personalised for users identified as children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold data that identifies you, most requests are handled by your device settings, the in-app privacy options, or by uninstalling the app. For data held by Google, see the links above. You can also contact us and we will help.

Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Personalised ads and ad measurement: your consent. Non-personalised ads, crash reports and basic analytics: our legitimate interest in keeping the app free, stable and improving it.

Retention

On-device data stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Firebase Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months; Crashlytics reports for up to 90 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
